ShadowProtocol

A social deduction card game for 4–8 players. Hackers upload data. Agents crash the system. Nobody knows who is who.

or join a friend

How to play

Roles. Most players are Hackers. A few are secret Corporate Agents, and the Agents know each other.

Your turn. Optionally play one Action card face-up, then upload one card face-down to the pile. When everyone has uploaded, the pile is shuffled and revealed. Nobody can tell who uploaded what.

Scoring. Data Nodes score for the Hackers, Malware scores for the Agents. Every other card counts as Junk Code. Hackers need 6 Data Nodes. Agents need 3 to 5 Malware depending on the player count.

Elimination. Hackers also win by disconnecting every Agent. Agents also win once they equal the Hackers in number.

Action cards. Scan peeks at a random card in someone's hand. Firewall blocks the next Action aimed at you. Overclock makes a player cycle two cards. Trace disconnects a player, but burns one of your cards and everyone sees who did it. Audit starts a vote. A majority disconnects a player, and ejecting an innocent Hacker costs the Hackers a Data Node.
